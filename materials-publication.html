Interactive publication · CCT305

Materials

An illustrated digital publication about how surfaces bring game worlds to life.

Make the contents interactive

The fixed-layout EPUB uses material spheres as visual links to sections. The contents page lets the reader select a surface and move to its chapter.

Image-led layouts carry the theme across 17 screens. Articles, embedded media, and credits each have a place in the publication.

What it uses

  • Adobe InDesign
  • Fixed-layout EPUB
  • Interactive editorial navigation

Credits

The publication showcases layout and interaction design using credited editorial material.
