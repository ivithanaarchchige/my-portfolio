3D environment · CCT285

Replica

An immersive house replica where you have four minutes to pack your books and catch the bus.

Recreating a place in 3D

Search the house for books, collect them in your backpack, and get out the front door. Doors, boxes and other everyday objects become part of the route through the house.

Bedrooms, a washroom, and a kitchen give the player a familiar setting to navigate from a first-person view.

What it uses

  • Unity
  • 3D scene composition
  • Environmental modeling

Project format

The coursework was supplied as a Windows build.
