Browser game

Hand-Tracked Air Hockey

Two players move paddles with their fingertips in a camera-controlled air hockey experiment.

Play air hockey

Hands as controllers

The original sketch tracks two index fingertips and maps them to paddles. Players protect their goals while trying to send the puck past the other side.

The browser demo also offers keyboard and touch controls. Use W, A, S, D for one paddle and the arrow keys for the other. The camera mode runs only when you choose it and grant permission.

What it uses

  • JavaScript and p5.js
  • ml5.js hand tracking
  • Collision and scorekeeping
