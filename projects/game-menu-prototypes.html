Game Menu Prototypes

A Figma study for how players move from a game’s start screen to modes and video settings.

Open in Figma

Planning the player’s first clicks

The study covers the start screen, mode selection, settings, and video settings. A monochrome hierarchy keeps the interface aligned with the game’s visual direction.

The write-up records a Figma issue found during iteration. The video settings switches changed variants in the editor but behaved differently in presentation mode.
