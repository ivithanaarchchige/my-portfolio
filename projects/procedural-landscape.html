Simple Drawing

A sunset mountain landscape that redraws its ridgelines from randomized points.

The project

The drawing builds depth from three overlapping mountain silhouettes. The underlying rules stay consistent across renders, while random vertex heights give each new landscape a different skyline.

What it uses

  • JavaScript and p5.js
  • Vertex-based shapes
  • Procedural drawing
