Promotional design · CCT305

Steam Deck Poster

An unofficial poster study that presents the Steam Deck through a bold headline and a single focal image.

The project

The design uses a dark background, high-contrast type, and a large console photograph to organize the reading order. The headline lands first, then the product and its supporting details.

What it uses

  • Adobe InDesign
  • Promotional layout
  • Typographic hierarchy

Credits

Unofficial coursework concept. The source submission credits the product photo to IGN and product branding to Steam and Valve.
