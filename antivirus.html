Unity prototype · CCT382

Antivirus

A first-person action prototype that sends the player inside a computer to confront a Trojan Horse boss.

Prototyping movement and objectives

The prototype uses connected rooms, health pickups, and enemy encounters to guide the player toward a red elevator and the Trojan Horse. The player follows the objective through the level.

What it uses

  • Unity
  • First-person movement
  • Room-to-room objectives

Project format

The supplied build targets Windows.
