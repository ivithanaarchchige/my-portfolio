Arcade game · CCT211

Asteroid Dodger

A scrolling space shooter with asteroid hazards, two ship styles, and three lives.

The project

A scrolling backdrop sets the pace while asteroids cross the play area. The ship can fire, move around incoming hazards, and take hits before losing its last life.

Sprites mark the difference between the ship, its projectiles, the asteroids, and the hearts in the health display.

What it uses

  • Python and Pygame
  • Sprites and scrolling backgrounds
  • Collision and lives
