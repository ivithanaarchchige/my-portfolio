About me

At the University of Toronto Mississauga, I studied Communication, Culture, Information and Technology alongside Technology, Code and Society. Drawing a character on my laptop opened up a new interest in visual work. In my coursework, that interest found its way into everything from illustrated game worlds to a mushroom-themed website.

  • Game development with Unity, Ren’Py, and Pygame
  • Interactive prototypes with JavaScript, p5.js, and Figma
  • Visual communication through illustration, layout, and data visualization
